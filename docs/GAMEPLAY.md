# Gameplay system

## Core fantasy

You are not “placing a sportsbook bet.” You are entering a token battle against the other side of the internet.

**Loop:** acquire BALL → pick a game → choose a side → choose burn mode → wager → watch supply disappear → claim if right → climb the sewer → repeat.

## Status systems

### Burn modes
- NORMAL — 5%
- HOT — 10%
- UNWELL — 25%
- MARTYR — 50%

Extra burn never increases payout odds. It is destructive status spending.

### Rat Score (offchain/indexed)
Suggested first scoring model:
- bet placed: +5
- winning market: +25
- underdog at lock wins: +50
- every 100k BALL burned: +1, capped per market
- 3-win streak: +25
- 5-win streak: +75
- 7-win streak: +200

Do not award token emissions for XP. Keep BALL fixed supply.

### Titles
- 0–99: Sewer Tourist
- 100–299: Rat
- 300–599: Degenerate
- 600–899: Sewer Lord
- 900+: Needs Help

### Retention surfaces
- The Incinerator — lifetime burn leaderboard
- Hall of Shame — biggest realized losses / coldest streaks
- Sewer League — weekly XP leaderboard
- Upset Board — wallets that repeatedly back minority pools and win
- Game of the Sewer — one featured event with oversized burn presentation

## Daily missions
Use non-financial XP/badges initially:
- enter 3 pools
- back one minority side
- burn 100k BALL
- participate in two sports
- claim one win

## Protocol loop
Every 100 BALL at base settings:
- 5 BALL burned
- 2 BALL treasury
- 93 BALL effective pool

Treasury policy should be separately disclosed. Do not imply treasury-held BALL “backs” the token.
